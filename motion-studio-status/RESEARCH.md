# Research – WhatsApp Status "Unlock" video

**Direction.** Premium night-sky palette (deep indigo + the logo's rainbow spectrum + gold), bold geometric display type, and kinetic, beat-led motion built around one motif: the key from the logo's tagline, *"Education is the Key!"*.

**What the best do, and how this video uses it**
- Kinetic typography leads 2026 short-form because most viewers watch with the sound off, so text carries the message. Every scene here is led by big animated type.
- Vertical-first design: built natively at 1080×1920 with key content kept out of the top 250 px and bottom 350 px.
- Scale-contrast, beat-synced type: huge single words (OPEN, CBSE, STATE BOARD) cut on the music.
- A strong 0–2 s hook: a key unlocks a glowing keyhole before any information appears.

**How we beat generic admission posters-in-motion**
- One signature motif (the key and keyhole) opens and closes the video.
- Meaningful transitions: keyhole iris → colour floods per board → whip-push → zoom through the map pin.
- Count-ups (5th → 10th, 5th → 12th) that tick on every number, with a matching sound tick.
- Designed sound effects placed on the exact frames from the code.

**Platform.** WhatsApp Status video limits are reported at 60–90 s, with a 16 MB size cap in one guide. This video is 17 s and about 9.6 MB.
