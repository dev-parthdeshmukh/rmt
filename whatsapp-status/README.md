# Rapid Mind's Tutorials – 10 s WhatsApp Status (Admissions)

**Video:** [`rapid-minds-admissions-status.mp4`](rapid-minds-admissions-status.mp4) – 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 10 s, with music

The design looks like a page from a spiral notebook. Marker handwriting spells out **ADMISSIONS OPEN!** with a yellow highlighter swipe. Next comes a ticked checklist with highlighter colours for **CBSE** (Classes 5th–10th), **State Board** (Classes 5th–12th) and **Commerce** (11th & 12th Std.). A yellow sticky note slaps on with the address (First Floor, Lagad Pride, Line Ali, Old Panvel, Maharashtra 410206). Finally, a red pen circles **Enroll Now!**. The logo is clipped on as a polaroid.

The music is a cheerful ukulele-style strum with snaps and glockenspiel. Paper and pen sound effects are timed to the animation. It is synthesized in `source/music.py`.

Re-render: `cd source && python3 music.py && node render.js video video_noaudio.mp4 30 10`, then add `music.wav` with ffmpeg (`loudnorm=I=-14`).
Fonts: Permanent Marker (Apache 2.0) and Caveat (SIL OFL).
